AMULET ATLAS — AGENT BRIEF (shared by every drafting agent)
==========================================================
Repo: /Users/annikapeacock/Developer/claude code projects/amulet-atlas
Every path below is relative to it.

Corpus: plain-text Wikipedia articles, one per file, each carrying its title, URL and
fetch date at the top, in the folder your launching brief names. tools/fetch_wiki.py
rebuilds it. Every article in there already has a source id in
data/sources/sources.json, in the form s:wp-<filename-without-.txt>.

READ FIRST, IN THIS ORDER
  1. AUTHORING.txt             the record spec, the voice rules, and the full cast list
  2. schema/node.schema.json   what a record must look like
  3. The three exemplars, written by hand. Match their depth, their voice and their
     provenance discipline:
       data/nodes/amulet/wedjat-eye.json    a rich amulet: dating, object, against, 9 kin
       data/nodes/motif/eye.json            a sign that crosses traditions
       data/nodes/place/tha-phrachan.json   a place: geo, address, hours, tags
  4. data/vocab/*.json          regions, against, materials, forms, worn, museums,
                                facets, tags, recognizers, types

WHAT YOU DO
  Write the records in YOUR batch, one JSON file per record at
  data/nodes/<type>/<id>.json. Nothing else. Do not touch tools/, do not edit
  data/sources/sources.json, do not edit another agent's records.

SOURCES
  Cite only ids that exist in data/sources/sources.json, OR add your own to
  data/sources/new-<your-batch-name>.json in this shape:
    {"sources":[{"id":"s:web-slug","kind":"web","title":"...","publisher":"...",
                 "url":"https://...","accessed":"2026-09-16"}]}
  build.py merges those in. For anything not in the corpus, use WebFetch, READ the page,
  and register it with its real title, publisher, URL and the date you read it.
  NEVER invent a source, a URL, an ISBN, a date, an accession number, a price, a quote,
  a measurement or a death. An accession number with no catalogue URL is a hard error.

PROVENANCE, WHICH IS THE POINT OF THE PROJECT
  provenance.default.tier plus per-field overrides. Tiers:
    cited      a named source in sources[] backs this
    harvested  a tool fetched it from an open dataset or an open collection
    tradition  general knowledge of the practice; hedge it in the prose
    inference  this project reasoning from the above; say so in the prose
    field      somebody stood there (nothing carries this yet)
  In text.story mark each stretch inline: plain prose = cited; *Tradition holds —* ;
  *Inference —*. A fact you cannot source goes in hedged or stays out.
  Set needs_verification: true and confidence "medium"/"low" where you are unsure.

THE THREE SENTENCES AN AMULET RECORD OWES A READER
  1. What it IS — physically, so a reader could pick it out of a tray.
  2. What it is FOR — in the words of people who carry it, attributed by name in
     against[] and in the prose. Name the claimant; then stop. Whether a charm works is
     not a question these pages answer.
  3. WHERE and WHEN — countries[] as ISO codes and dating{} in astronomical years
     (1 CE = 1, 1 BCE = 0, 500 BCE = -499). Those two fields ARE the map and the
     timeline. A record without them is invisible on both.

VOICE (house rules; validate.py enforces the banned list as a hard error)
  BANNED: superstition, superstitious, primitive, savage, backward, witch doctor,
  mumbo jumbo, hocus pocus, trinket, knick-knack, exotic, authentic, inauthentic,
  genuine article, the real thing, merely, mere, so-called, voodoo. A quotation in
  quotation marks is exempt — quote and attribute instead of asserting. (The tradition
  is Vodou or Vodun; the two-o spelling belongs inside a quotation or a proper noun.)
  "Is believed to" with nobody named raises a warning. Name who says it.
  Every argument in this field gets REPORTED and none gets JOINED: factory bead against
  village bead, airport dreamcatcher against Ojibwe one, Thai amulet market as devotion
  or as trade. Both sides, both names, their own words.
  Write short. Lead with a verb. Define every non-obvious word at first use. Prices carry
  their date and currency. No sentence that announces the next one. No closing summary.

THE RULE THAT OUTRANKS COMPLETENESS
  Where a tradition restricts an object, a design or a rite to initiates, to one gender,
  to one clan or to the dead, this atlas prints the restriction and stops. Use the
  `restricted` block, name who says so, and do not write the detail anywhere else in the
  record. Do not reconstruct a guarded rite from scattered sources and do not fill a gap
  with a neighbouring tradition's practice. A blank with a citation beats a full record
  built on a guess.

KIN IS THE SITE'S SPINE
  Each kin entry says what the other node IS to this one, in one sentence a reader
  standing on this page would want. Not "see also". The other record answers in its own
  words, so write yours from here. Link ONLY to ids on the cast list in AUTHORING.txt.
  5-12 kin on a rich record, 3-6 on a small one. Reach across batches freely — the other
  agent is writing that record right now.

PICTURES
  images[] stays empty. Put candidate Wikimedia Commons file titles in
  x_commons_files: ["File:...jpg"] and a later pass fetches them with their licences.

FINISH BY
  running, from the repo root:  BUILD_DRAFT=1 python3 tools/validate.py
  Fix every ERROR that names one of YOUR records. Kin-target warnings for records other
  agents are still writing are expected and fine.
  Then report: how many records you wrote, which cast-list ids you could NOT source and
  why, and every fact you had to hedge.
