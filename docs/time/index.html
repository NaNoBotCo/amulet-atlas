Amulet AtlasThe timeline

241 records with a date on them, from the oldest to the one somebody bought this morning. The axis is not linear, and the ticks say so: five thousand years at one scale would give the last two centuries about a millimetre, and the last two centuries are where a third of these records live. Every tick is printed so you can see the stretching happen.

That curve is the shape of this atlas, not of the past. It rises at 1800 because the written record rises at 1800.

Every dated record

A bar with an open circle at its right-hand end is something people still carry. A dotted bar is a date somebody reasoned to rather than dug up. Colour is the era the record files itself under.

Undated, and why that matters

Astronomical years: 1 CE is 1, 1 BCE is 0, 500 BCE is -499. A row with no end year is still carried. `method` says how the date is known — an excavated date and an ethnographer's date are not the same kind of fact.
