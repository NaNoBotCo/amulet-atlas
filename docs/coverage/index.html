Amulet AtlasWhere we stop

Amulets, charms and talismans worldwide — what they are made of, where on the body or the house they go, what their carriers say they stop, and when and where each one is documented. Every hemisphere, from the Neolithic to a sticker on a phone.

How lopsided this still is

Every record counts once per hemisphere it touches, so the four numbers add to more than the record count. The northern and eastern bars are long because the written record this atlas reads from is long there. That is a fact about the sources, not about where people carry charms.
