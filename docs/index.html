Every hemisphere, from a bead somebody drilled five thousand years ago to a sticker on a phone. Each record says what the thing is, what its carriers say it stops — named, quoted, and never tested here — and where and when it is documented. Where a tradition asks that something not be published, this atlas prints the ask instead of the thing.
